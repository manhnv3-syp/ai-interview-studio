# Code of Conduct

Interview Studio welcomes respectful collaboration from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Discuss ideas and code without harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, or publication of another person's private information.

Contributors and maintainers should give specific, constructive feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and protect the privacy of people reporting a concern. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation when needed to keep the project usable and safe.

Do not put sensitive conduct reports in public issues. This checkout does not yet have a project-owned private reporting channel; maintainers should configure one before opening a public community and publish its details here.
